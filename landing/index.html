Founder

Scott Harper

Chief Executive Officer

Scott Harper is a tech entrepreneur and investor who has spent two decades building, scaling, and selling technology companies. He is Co-Founder and CEO of MEGA, where he is applying modern AI to specialty insurance.

Before MEGA, Scott co-founded and served as CEO of Dialexa, a product engineering firm he bootstrapped to more than 350 engineers, strategists, and designers before IBM acquired it in 2022. He led the Dialexa business and IBM’s product engineering efforts through 2024. Scott was named EY Entrepreneur of the Year and holds a BS in Mechanical Engineering with a Biomedical Specialization from SMU. He lives in Dallas, TX with his wife and three children.
